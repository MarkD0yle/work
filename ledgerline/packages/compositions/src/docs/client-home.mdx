# Client Home

**Status:** Hypothesis. Its acceptance criteria pass in automated tests. No user has tested it yet, so it has not moved to Draft (SPEC §11).

**Spec:** SPEC 8.6

## Purpose

What an external client needs on one page: their positions, their transactions and their statements.

## When to use, and when not

**Use it** as the landing page for users in the client segment.

**Do not use it** for internal users, who have the workbench.

## Foundations

No job tray, no bulk actions, no admin. The latest month-end statement is one click from sign-in.

## States

Loading, no statements yet (says when the first will appear), statements listed, download in progress, and out of scope (refused with the reason).

## Data contract

`PositionRow`, `Statement`, `TransactionRow`. Everything is limited to the user's blotter scope by the server.

## Permissions

`transaction.view` and `export.run`. A record outside the scope is refused with "outside your account scope".

## Accessibility

Each download button names the portfolio and the month. The page states which portfolios are on the account.
