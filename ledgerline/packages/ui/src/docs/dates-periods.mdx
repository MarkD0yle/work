# Dates and Periods

**Status:** Hypothesis. Its acceptance criteria pass in automated tests. No user has tested it yet, so it has not moved to Draft (SPEC §11).

**Spec:** SPEC 7.6

## Purpose

Business dates with a stated basis (trade, settle, effective, ledger, valuation), entered quickly and checked against market calendars and accounting periods.

## When to use, and when not

**Use it** for any business date. Use the period picker for month-end periods.

**Do not use it** for timestamps (use `formatTimestamp`), and never with JavaScript `Date` for a business date.

## Foundations

Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` strings. Null is null, never a placeholder date. Shortcuts (`t`, `t+2`, `eom`) count from the engine's business date, not the browser clock.

## States

Empty, valid, adjusted (moved off a weekend or holiday, with the rule named), warning (kept on a non-business day), blocked (a closed period, naming the period and who can reopen it) and unreadable.

## Data contract

`BusinessDate`: value, basis, optional calendar and business-day rule. `AccountingPeriod` carries a state per close type.

## Permissions

Entry into a closed period is refused in the field and again by the server. Reopening needs the reopen permission and a second approver.

## Accessibility

Every date field shows its basis as text. The resolved date is spelled out under the field as the user types. Errors are announced and stay until fixed.
